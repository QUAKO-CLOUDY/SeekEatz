### Description

SeekEatz helps you find restaurant meals that fit your goals before you order.

Search naturally for meals by calories, protein, food type, restaurant, or dietary preference. SeekEatz uses real restaurant nutrition data and AI-assisted retrieval to surface meal options that are actually available.

Whether you're trying to eat higher protein, stay under a calorie target, or compare better options at a specific restaurant, SeekEatz helps you make the decision faster.

Key features:

- Search restaurant meals by calories, protein, carbs, fats, and dietary preferences
- Ask naturally for what you want, like “high protein under 600 calories” or “best Chick-fil-A meals under 500 calories”
- Get meal cards from real restaurant menus instead of generic suggestions
- Use AI-powered meal swaps and modifier suggestions where supported
- Save favorite meals and log meals you choose
- Compare options across many restaurants from one place

SeekEatz is built for people who want fast, practical restaurant decisions without digging through menu PDFs or nutrition charts.

Important:

- Nutrition values can vary by restaurant preparation, customization, and menu updates
- SeekEatz is for informational purposes only
- SeekEatz does not provide medical or nutrition advice
