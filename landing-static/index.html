The Problem

Eating out should not be hard to track.

Every meal out is a gamble without the right info. Most people give up tracking the moment they leave home.

Restaurant menus rarely list macros Most menues show only the dish name and ingredients. No macros. No real breakdown. Just names and prices.
Nutritional data is not always transparent The information exists, but it’s not accessible or usable in the moment.
Finding nutrition mid-order is a mess and time consuming. Digging through websites or PDFs while you’re waiting or with company doesn’t work.
You feel full but there’s still food on the plate and feel obligated to finish So you log it after the fact and hope it didn’t throw off your day.
You guess… and hope for the best Second guessing if you overate or didn’t hit your goals.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is SeekEatz?

SeekEatz is an AI-powered meal recommendation app that searches real restaurant menus for meals matching your calorie and macro goals.

How does the AI search work?

Share your calories, macros, cravings, or preferences with the AI concierge and get matching meal cards in your area that fit those parameters.

How do we get our data?

We pull nutrition data directly from official restaurant PDFs, verified nutrition menus, and a wide range of vetted databases.

How many restaurants are available?

We currently have over a couple hundred restaurants available with over 10,000 menu items to choose from.

Is SeekEatz free?

Free — Access AI chat search with up to 3 chats per day. Monthly subscription — Unlock all features, including unlimited chat, filters, and tracking. Lifetime — One-time payment for full access to all features and future updates.

What diet types are supported?

We support high-protein, low-carb, keto, vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, or a fully custom setup tailored to you. Set your own macro targets and find meals that fit.
